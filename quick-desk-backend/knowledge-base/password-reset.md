# Password Reset Policy

## How to Reset Your Password

**Option 1 - Self Reset:**
1. Go to the company accounts portal
2. Click Forgot Password
3. Enter your company email
4. Check your personal email for reset link
5. Click the link and set a new password

**Option 2 - IT Support:**
Contact your IT manager directly for manual password reset.
IT team resets within 2 business hours.

## Password Requirements
- Minimum 8 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character
- Cannot reuse last 3 passwords

## Important Notes
- Passwords expire every 90 days
- Never share your password with anyone including IT team
- If you suspect your account is compromised contact IT manager immediately

## Account Locked?
After 5 wrong attempts your account gets locked.
Contact your IT manager directly to unlock.

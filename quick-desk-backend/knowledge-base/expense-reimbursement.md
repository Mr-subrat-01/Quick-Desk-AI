# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## What Can Be Claimed
- Travel expenses for official work
- Client meeting meals
- Office supplies purchased personally
- Work from home internet allowance up to 500 per month
- Training and certification fees with prior approval

## How to Submit Claim
1. Login to the company finance portal
2. Click New Expense Claim
3. Fill expense details and amount
4. Upload receipts mandatory for amounts above 500 rupees
5. Submit for manager approval

## Approval and Payment
- Manager approves within 2 business days
- Finance team processes within 5 business days after approval
- Amount credited to your salary account

## Important Rules
- Claims must be submitted within 30 days of expense
- Original receipts required for amounts above 500 rupees
- No reimbursement for personal expenses
- Travel above 5000 rupees needs prior approval

## Rejected Claims
If claim rejected you will get reason via email.
Fix the issue and resubmit.
Contact your Finance manager directly for further queries.

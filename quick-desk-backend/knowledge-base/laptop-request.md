# Laptop Request and Repair Policy

## New Laptop Request
New laptop requests are approved based on:
- Your role and department requirements
- Current availability in inventory
- Manager approval

**Steps:**
1. Email your manager with reason and department
2. Manager forwards request to IT team
3. IT team delivers within 3-5 business days after approval

## Laptop Repair Request
If your laptop has hardware issues:
1. Inform your IT manager describing the issue
2. IT team will assess within 24 hours
3. If repairable fixed within 2-3 days
4. If not repairable replacement will be arranged

## Battery Issues
Battery replacement is covered if:
- Battery drains within 2 hours on full charge
- Battery not charging properly
- Laptop shutting down randomly

Contact IT manager with your laptop model and issue description.
Battery replacement done within 48 hours.

## Accessories
For keyboard mouse charger requests contact your Admin manager directly.

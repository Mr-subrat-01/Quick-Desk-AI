# Office Supplies Request

## Available Supplies
- Stationery such as pens notebooks markers sticky notes
- Printer cartridges and paper
- Desk accessories such as organizers and mouse pads
- Cables and basic peripherals

## How to Request
1. Email your Admin manager with items and quantity needed
2. Admin team delivers within 1-2 business days

## Work From Home Supplies
WFH employees can request:
- Basic stationery once per quarter
- Cables and USB accessories with manager approval
- Ergonomic accessories with doctor recommendation

## Printer Issues
If office printer is not working:
1. Note the printer location and error message
2. Contact your IT manager directly
3. IT team responds within 2 hours

## Bulk Orders
For bulk orders for team events or workshops:
- Request at least 1 week in advance
- Mention event date and estimated quantity
- Admin manager approval required for orders above 5000 rupees

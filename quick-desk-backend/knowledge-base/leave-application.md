# Leave Application Process

## Types of Leave
- Casual Leave: 12 days per year
- Sick Leave: 8 days per year
- Annual Leave: 18 days per year
- Emergency Leave: Based on situation and manager approval

## How to Apply
1. Login to the company HR portal
2. Click Apply Leave
3. Select leave type and dates
4. Add reason
5. Submit - manager gets notified automatically

## Approval Process
- Manager approves or rejects within 24 hours
- You get email notification on approval or rejection
- Approved leave reflects in your leave balance immediately

## Important Rules
- Apply at least 2 days before for planned leaves
- Sick leave requires medical certificate if more than 2 days
- Emergency leave can be applied same day
- Leaves during project deadlines require manager discussion

## Leave Balance
Check your leave balance anytime on the company HR portal.
Contact your HR manager directly for leave policy queries.

# VPN Setup Guide

## How to Connect
1. Download the VPN client from the company software portal
2. Install and open the application
3. Enter the server address provided by your IT team
4. Login with your company email and password
5. Click Connect

## Common Issues

**VPN not connecting:**
Check your internet connection first. Disconnect and reconnect.
If still not working restart your computer and try again.

**Wrong password error:**
Your VPN password is same as your company email password.
If you recently changed your email password use the new one.

**Slow connection:**
Disconnect and reconnect. If issue persists restart your system.

**Cannot find server:**
Make sure you entered the server address correctly.
Disable any other VPN or proxy running on your system.

## Note
VPN is required when accessing any company tools from outside office.
Contact your IT manager directly for urgent VPN access issues.
